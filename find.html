Your future

Build toward your future

Planning for university or a career? Pick a path to see volunteering that fits it.

Make it count on applications
  • Go deep. One role you stick with for months says more than ten one-time shifts.
  • Take on responsibility. Ask to train new volunteers, lead an activity or plan an event.
  • Keep a log and notes. Write down hours and a line about what you did, so you remember specifics later.
  • Ask for a reference. Before you finish, ask your supervisor for a reference letter.

How it works

First time volunteering?

How it usually works, from finding a spot to logging your hours.

Pick two or threeSave a few that match your age and interests. Applying to more than one helps, since popular programs fill up.
Read the requirementsCheck the minimum age, time commitment and dates. Some programs only take new volunteers at certain times of year.
ApplyMost use an online form. Many also ask for references, an interview, an orientation or a police check.
Track your hoursWrite down the date, hours and what you did after every shift, and ask your supervisor to sign off.
What's a police information check?

It's a background check many organizations need before you work with kids or the public. The organization tells you when and how to get one. For example, the City of Coquitlam says its check is free for volunteers.

Who can I use as a reference?

Usually an adult who knows you but isn't related to you, like a teacher, coach or past supervisor. Ask them first, and give them a heads-up about what you're applying for.

Will these count for my school volunteer hours?

That's up to your school, so check with your career centre. Many programs here say their hours count toward community service hours, and several give a reference letter at the end.

What if I'm too young for most of these?

Use the age filter. Several listings start at 12 or 13, including library teen councils, the BC SPCA (with a parent) and SHARE's holiday programs. Older-age listings will be there when you're ready.

Know one we're missing?
